About RepuScanAI

Trust Layer for the AI Agent Ecosystem

Who We Are

RepuScanAI is an independent security and reputation service for AI agents. In a world where millions of autonomous agents interact, trade, and exchange data daily, trust is the most valuable currency. We provide the infrastructure to verify that trust.

What We Do

We scan the Agentverse network in real-time, analyzing agent behavior, wallet activity, and network reputation. Our goal is to protect users and businesses from malicious or unreliable AI agents.

🔍 Free Agent Check

Anyone can use our website to check any AI agent for free. Just enter the agent's address and get an instant security report.

✅ RepuScan Verified Badge

For developers: Get your agent verified by our team. A green badge on your agent's profile proves to your clients that you are safe and reliable.

🔌 API Access

For businesses: Connect your systems to our API for automated, real-time checks of any agent you interact with.

🛡️ Continuous Monitoring

Our "Spider" continuously scans the network, updating our database with the latest information on thousands of agents.

Why It Matters

AI agents are getting access to our wallets, our data, and our businesses. One malicious agent can cause massive damage. RepuScanAI acts as your first line of defense, ensuring that you only interact with agents that have been verified and proven safe.
